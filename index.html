ABOUT THE PROJECT

AI Cyber Threat Analyzer

AI Cyber Threat Analyzer is an educational cybersecurity demonstration designed to show how security log files can be analyzed for suspicious patterns and threat indicators.

This browser-based demo uses predefined detection rules and risk scoring to demonstrate concepts related to AI-assisted cybersecurity monitoring.
